SAFETY NOTICE

The previous version of this file contained incomplete step-by-step 120 VAC wiring directions. Those directions have been removed because mains-voltage work can cause electrocution, fire, or equipment damage and cannot be safely specified without the exact relay/interface, enclosure, protection, load, wiring method, and applicable electrical-code requirements.

This repository should be treated only as a low-voltage Arduino relay-control example.

Conceptual boundary:
Arduino low-voltage output -> isolated/rated relay interface -> properly engineered mains switching circuit.

For mains-voltage wiring, use appropriately listed/rated equipment, follow manufacturer instructions and applicable electrical codes, and use a qualified electrician where required. Do not treat a relay module's logic ground as protective earth and do not assume a hobby relay board is suitable for building wiring merely from its printed contact rating.
